## Brand & Style

This design system serves independent educators, coaching tutors, and boutique institute owners across India. The aesthetic blends the authoritative gravitas of institutional academia with the frictionless velocity of modern consumer fintech. It balances structured discipline with approachability, positioning software not as administrative overhead, but as an indispensable operating companion.

### Design Movement: Modern Academic SaaS
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** Built on deep indigo foundations that evoke discipline, knowledge, and institutional reliability.
- **Action-Oriented & Crisp:** High-clarity interactive surfaces utilizing vivid royal blue triggers, high-legibility currency figures, and immediate tactile feedback.
- **Contextually Grounded (India-First):** Native ergonomic accommodation for WhatsApp communication channels, UPI transactional tracking, vernacular-friendly typographic scaling, and high-density student batch management under sub-optimal network or lighting conditions.
- **Subtle AI Sophistication:** AI copilot capabilities (question paper generators, attendance auto-summaries, diagnostic analytics) are delineated through luminous violet-indigo gradients rather than intrusive gimmicks.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unambiguous hierarchy between administrative authority, primary interactions, domain-specific state indicators, and intelligent capabilities.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Deep Academic Indigo (`#1E3A8A` / `#0F172A`):** Anchors headers, primary navigation frames, critical stat headings, and system-level authority.
- **Interactive Royal Blue (`#2563EB`):** Dedicated to user actions: primary buttons, active segmented tabs, selected states, and progressive link navigations.
- **AI Violet / Intelligence (`#7C3AED` to `#8B5CF6`):** Reserved exclusively for automated suggestions, AI test generation, insight callouts, and magic actions.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, low-strain neutral ground that prevents optical fatigue during prolonged batch grading and schedule planning.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast, clean card faces and active elevations.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline container separation maintaining structural discipline without heavy borders.

### Functional & Regional Accent Roles
- **Success & Financial Positive (`#059669`):** Full fee clearance, verified attendance, on-time submissions, and UPI collection confirmations.
- **WhatsApp Direct (`#25D366`):** Dedicated utility accent for broadcast updates, parent notices, and single-tap message dispatches.
- **Warning & Pending (`#D97706`):** Partial payments, pending approvals, and upcoming fee grace periods.
- **Danger & At-Risk (`#DC2626`):** Defaulter flags, critical drop in attendance, overdue accounts, and batch schedule conflicts.

## Typography

The typography system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers, statistics, and brand touchpoints, alongside **Inter** for dense transactional records, student rosters, forms, and small-screen readability.

### Financial and Metric Hierarchy
- The `stat-currency` token is optimized specifically for Indian Rupee (`₹`) rendering. Tabular numerals (`tnum`) must be enforced for all currency listings, batch roll numbers, and percentage metrics to guarantee vertical alignment in lists and billing overviews.
- Headers maintain tight tracking to conserve horizontal screen space on common 360px–390px viewport widths.
- Section tags (`label-sm`, all-caps) use expanded letter spacing for quick glanceability in high-density operational views.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is strictly mobile-first, designed for single-hand portrait execution by tutors moving between physical classrooms or reviewing operations on the go.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid view. Safe horizontal margin of `1rem` (16px). Dynamic sticky bottom navigation and thumb-zone operational bars.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid. Gutters and margins at `1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates dual-pane views (e.g., student roster on the left, fee ledger on the right).
- **Desktop (≥ 1025px):** 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1280px`. Left-rail vertical navigation replacing the mobile bottom bar.

### Ergonomics
- The base rhythm uses an 8pt increment scale, with a 4pt sub-grid (`space-xs`) for micro-badges, icons, and currency counters.
- Interactive controls must maintain a minimum physical touch target of 44×44px (`touch-target-min`), regardless of visual icon size.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through crisp, low-opacity layered shadows with slight cool-slate tinting (`#0F172A`), keeping the interface lightweight and legible even on lower-brightness mobile displays.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Canvas backdrop (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Surface cards, student summary tiles, and batch strips.  
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`  
  Coupled with a hairline outline: `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0;`
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Active Touch):** Segmented controls, active search suggestions, batch filter pickers, and active dragged list rows.  
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Floating Actions):** Bottom action sheets (e.g., "Collect Payment", "Mark Batch Attendance"), snackbars, and floating primary triggers.  
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06);`

### Glass Surfaces
Glassmorphism is restricted to sticky mobile header bars and floating bottom navigation anchors:  
`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8);`

## Shapes

The design system uses balanced modern rounding (Level 2). This provides a friendly, welcoming consumer feel without sacrificing the visual density necessary for administrative records.

- **Base Controls & Inputs (`rounded-md`):** 8px border radius for text inputs, dropdown triggers, and student profile thumbnails.
- **Cards & Data Modules (`rounded-lg`):** 12px to 16px radius for batch cards, payment summary containers, and test rosters.
- **Action Overlays & Drawers (`rounded-xl`):** 20px to 24px top-corner radius for mobile bottom sheets to guide downward swipe gestures.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for academic subject badges (e.g., "Class 10 CBSE", "NEET Repeater"), attendance indicators, status chips, and circular icon triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2563EB` with white text, 48px height on mobile, bold typography (`label-lg`), 8px corner radius.
- **Secondary:** White background, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text, hover/press tint of `#F1F5F9`.
- **WhatsApp Action:** Dedicated `#25D366` green background with pure white typography and official WhatsApp icon mark. Used for instantaneous payment reminders and parent notes.
- **AI Magic Action:** Gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #7C3AED, #2563EB)`) with white text and trailing star/sparkle icon.

### Status Pills & Context Badges
Constructed with a 10% tinted background and 90% saturated text:
- **Paid / Present:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` text.
- **Pending / Grace Period:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#B45309` text.
- **Overdue / Absent / At-Risk:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text.
- **Academic Stream (CBSE, ICSE, JEE, NEET):** `#EEF2FF` background, `#3730A3` text, hairline `#C7D2FE` border.

### Input Fields
- Minimum height of 48px to accommodate effortless thumb input.
- Default: Border `#CBD5E1` on `#FFFFFF` background.
- Focus: `#2563EB` border with a 3px soft halo (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).
- Prefix integration for Indian contexts: Static `+91` prefix for phone numbers, fixed `₹` symbol prefix for fee collection fields.

### Cards & Roster Strips
- **Student List Row:** Optimized for fast vertical scanning. Left-aligned student avatar with status dot, title indicating name and roll number, subtitle showing academic board tag, and right-aligned metric displaying attendance percentage or fee balance.
- **Batch Card:** Header featuring class title and badge, body indicating active strength (`38 / 40 Students`), progress indicator, and rapid-action footer (Attendance, Broadcast).

### Bottom Sheets (Mobile Drawers)
All primary workflows (e.g., recording cash/UPI fees, logging marks, or adding a student) open as bottom drawers with a top center drag-handle (`#CBD5E1`, 36px wide, 4px high) and primary actions fixed at the base above mobile navigation bars.
